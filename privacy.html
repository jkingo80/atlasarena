AtlasArena Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

AtlasArena does not collect, sell or share any personal information. We have no servers that store data about you.

Data on your device

Your player name (if you enter one), XP, coins, scores, unlocked items and settings are saved only on your device using standard iOS storage. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also clear it any time in Settings → Reset Progress.

Map guesses

In Easy mode, the app checks which country your pin is in using Apple's built-in map services (MapKit / Core Location geocoding). Only the map point you tapped is sent, never your own location. AtlasArena does not ask for or use your device's location.

Photos

Place photos are downloaded from Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation). Like any website, Wikimedia's servers receive standard connection information such as your IP address when the photo is downloaded. Their privacy policy is at foundation.wikimedia.org.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

AtlasArena has no sign-in, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

AtlasArena does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
